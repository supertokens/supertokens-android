# R26 / D11: refresh timeout and redirects

D11 approves platform-default timeouts for legacy URLConnection refresh entry points; explicit new APIs retain their ten-second deadline. Both modes manually follow at most 20 same-origin redirects, checking the captured owner before each request and after each response. Cross-origin redirects remain rejected; D12 scheme upgrades and disconnected-request replay are outside this change.

The published 0.5.4 JDK oracle follows 301–303 as GET and 307 as POST, but does not follow 308. D11 deliberately follows 308 as POST, subject to the same origin and owner checks.
